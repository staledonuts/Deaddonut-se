## A chill game about fishing for the big catch!
This is the first major game project we did at indiegame gamedev school.

A 4 week project in Unity. I pitched the game idea and worked with the others in my team to form the full game idea. 

- I made the Poster.
- I made most of the Environmental art.
- Underwater flora
- Sprite-shape Tiles
- Underwater fog
- Trees
- Mountains
- Sun
- Clouds
- Background art

I set up the Post-processing for the Level.
Coded the line mechanic.
Made the store boat and the store owner character "Sleazy Oskar".
