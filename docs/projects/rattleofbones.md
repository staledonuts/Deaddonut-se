## An old one.

> The dead rise, and the infamous Doctor Corpus is responsible! As you rise from your grave, a very annoyed 'Death' pushes a scythe upon you and goes on vacation. Not knowing who you are, you set out on an adventure to defeat Doctor Corpus and his army of medical abominations.

This is a project I started over 5 years ago, and it is still around in some capacity. Initially created in Unity, this Metroidvania is my love for the genre and pixel art made manifest.

Now it is in Godot with a much better codebase, using a global shader variable to pick the game's palette!

![RobScreenshot1.](images/images/rob_slash4.png?frames=19&fps=12)

I am using a specific set of grayscale colors as a lookup for the shader, which uses the global variable to return the correct colors.
