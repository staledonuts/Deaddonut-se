## A old one.

> The dead rise, and the infamous Doctor Corpus is responsible! As you rise from your grave a very annoyed 'Death' pushes a Scythe upon you and goes on vacation. Not knowing who you are you set out on an adventure to defeat Doctor Corpus and his army of medical abominations.

this is a project i started 5+ years ago and it is still around in some capacity. Initially created on Unity, this metroidvania is my love for the genre and pixelart made manifest.

Now it is in Godot with a much better code base and uses a global shader variable to pick the games palette!
